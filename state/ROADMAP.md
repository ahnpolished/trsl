# Roadmap

## Vision
trsl gives husbands who can't say something straight a way to say it anyway:
write it raw and unfiltered, trsl turns it into something sendable, and the
output is built to travel — dropped straight into iMessage, WhatsApp, or an
IG DM — with the original unfiltered version held back as something the wife
can unlock for $1.

## Phase 1 (current): prove the core translate+share loop works end-to-end as a real deployed/runnable product
Before anything else, one thing has to be true: a husband can open a real
URL, type a raw message, get back a translated, shareable output, and send
that link to someone who can actually open it. Not a mock, not a local
prototype — a deployed product a real person can use today. The $1 paywall,
growth mechanics, and retention all assume this loop already works; none of
them matter if it doesn't.

**North star for this phase:** one real husband sends one real trsl output
to one real wife via iMessage/WhatsApp/IG DM, unprompted, because it was
good enough to send.

## Later phases (not sequenced yet)
- Prove wife will pay ($1 unlock of the original message)
- Growth / virality (what makes it spread beyond the first sender)
