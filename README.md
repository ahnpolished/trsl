# trsl

## App idea: trsl (Translate)
App for husbands who want to say something directly but can't. You can say something super straight from your gut, unfiltered. we, our app, translate that and send it to your wife. 
Your wife can pay us $1 to see the original message. Use our app at your discretion

- Web app, mobile first
- Output needs to be easily shareable to iMessage, Whatsapp, Instagram DM, or others
  - OG ready
- Need to be fun, eye-catching, social media shareable
